## The verdict changes, and in the opposite direction to the assumption

Full-rank verdicts: **['FAIL', 'INDETERMINATE']**. Rank-8 verdicts: **['INDETERMINATE', 'PASS']**.

The full-rank form FAILs on `rho` (0.46-0.57 against a bar of 1.05). DSN's shipped rank-8 operator never FAILs, and at `delta=0.1` it PASSes on both statistics at every complement scaling. **So the harness's previously-reported DSN rejection was a statement about an operator DSN does not apply.**

This does not make DSN good. Stage 2 already measures it training worse per FLOP than plain SGD, and the negative-result document stands on three independent mechanism measurements. What it does mean is that stage 1's rejection was not evidence about DSN, and should not have been reported as if it were.

## Why the rank-restricted `rho` values should not be read

`rho` for the rank-8 operator runs into the tens of thousands, and falls monotonically as the complement scaling `c` rises. That is not a discovery, it is mutant G1 firing: `rho` contains `tr(P^-1 Sigma) / tr(P Sigma)`, which diverges as `P` is shrunk on one subspace relative to another, with no reference to what that subspace costs the objective. A rank-restricted operator *is* a subspace-damping operator, so it lands squarely in `rho`'s known blind spot -- the same one that scored 33.5 on a configuration training 2,264x worse than SGD.

The `gain` column is the more trustworthy half here, and it also clears the bar.

## Two things that are solid

**No truncation error.** Every rank-restricted row has a measured depth convergence of 0.000: a restricted `P` is an explicit operator, so its three `P`-dependent forms are computed by applying it, and the only quadrature left (`g'Hg`) is a degree-one polynomial that a Gauss rule integrates exactly at any depth. The full-rank `delta=1e-3` row, by contrast, has a convergence of 4.7 -- it is not a measurement.

**`nan` trajectory gains are the safeguard working.** A `nan` means the noisy-quadratic model went unbounded at some checkpoint (the denominator `g'PHPg + tr(PHP Sigma)` went non-positive on indefinite curvature, and `neg_frac` is 0.42 at step 0). Those rows are INDETERMINATE rather than PASS, which is the intended behavior: a broken model cannot be summed into a trajectory total.

## Rank is not the binding constraint here

The rank sweep at `delta=1e-2` moves non-monotonically and is driven by whether some checkpoint's quadratic model went unbounded, not by rank. On this trajectory the choice of `delta` and the complement scaling both matter more than `k`.
